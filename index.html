About this map

Study area for runoff, road drainage and erosion along the La Cueva corridor, Jemez Mountains, Sandoval County, New Mexico. Terrain layers cover a 3.3 × 3.9 km rectangle around the study corridor.

Data

  • Terrain: 2024 Jemez Mountains bare-earth lidar, 0.25 m (NAD83(2011) UTM 13N). Hillshade at 0.25 m; elevation colors from a 1 m DEM averaged from the lidar. Spot elevations and profiles use a 2 m grid.
  • Slope class: percent slope from the 1 m DEM (lightly smoothed), classed 0–10, 10–15, 15–25, 25–30, >30 %.
  • Drainage lines: D8 flow routing on the depression-filled 1 m DEM; lines drawn where ≥0.2 ha drains through, classed by contributing area.
  • Contours: 20 and 40 ft (US survey feet), from the 0.25 m DEM lightly smoothed.
  • Roads: state, county, municipal and private ownership from NMDOT (names from NM911 centerlines); Forest Service roads from the USFS Southwestern Region road database (INFRA). Bridges: NMDOT bridge inventory (structures >20 ft).
  • Photos: 16 original geotagged photos selected from the 213 taken on 27 Oct 2026 during a field walk with Tina Trout; photo IDs are their positions (001–213) in the full walk sequence. Walk groups and descriptions come from her transcript; they are contextual labels, not technical diagnoses.

Limits

  • Photo points are camera GPS positions, not surveyed feature locations; recorded GPS error for these photos is 2.7–13.8 m (shown in each photo's details). Arrows show the camera direction.
  • Contributing areas are counted only inside the terrain rectangle; streams entering from outside carry more water than shown.
  • Culverts are not represented in the drainage model, so road fills act as dams; flow paths near roads may be wrong.
  • "Municipal" road ownership is as coded by NMDOT and has not been verified.
  • Not survey-grade. For planning and discussion only.
